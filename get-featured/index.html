A big island for visitors, even if it’s not the Big Island

Oʻahu received 5.79 million visitors arriving by air in 2024, by far the most of any Hawaiian island, according to the state’s Data Book. In the first ten months of 2025 they spent $7.88 billion on the island, up 4.9%.

A small share of those visitors dive or snorkel, but they plan ahead and book online, which makes them easy to reach with a clear listing.

Questions owners ask

Is there a free option?

Yes. A basic listing on Best Diving in Oahu is free for dive shops and snorkel operators serving Oʻahu. We verify every operator first. Featured is an optional upgrade at $29 a month.

What exactly do I get with Featured?

A top spot in the Oʻahu dive and snorkel site guides that match your trips, a Featured badge, one photo, a short pitch in your words and a direct link to your booking page. You can swap the photo or pitch whenever your season or offer changes.

Will Featured guarantee bookings?

No. We promise the placement and the work behind it, not a number of bookings. Visitors still decide based on your reviews, price, safety record and how easy you are to book.

How is a paid spot labeled?

Every paid placement shows a visible Featured badge, and the link to your site is marked rel="sponsored". That keeps the guide honest for readers and within search engine rules, and it protects your own site too.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime online. Your spot stays live through the month you’ve paid for and then returns to a basic listing.
